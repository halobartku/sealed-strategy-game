How to play (one paragraph)

Eight lots (Sun ☼, Moon ☸, Star ★) are auctioned one at a time to 2–4 players who each start with 100 coins. Everyone secretly values the suits differently — exactly one suit is worth double points to you — and bids are sealed: all bids are revealed together, the highest pays their bid and takes the lot, and ties go to the earlier seat. Highest total points after 8 lots wins; leftover coins break ties. Every turn you weigh the lot's base value against your hidden multiplier, what the others might be hiding, and how many coins you'll need for the lots still to come — spend everything early and you're powerless at the end.
